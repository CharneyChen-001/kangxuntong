Quality and test

VNA tuning, a report with the shipment, PIM when it is ordered.

What is measured

Each unit is tuned on a vector network analyzer. The outgoing report states insertion loss, ripple, VSWR and the rejection or isolation points on the datasheet, at room temperature in a 50 Ω system.

Low PIM

When the order calls for the low-PIM grade, PIM3 is tested at 2×43 dBm against the ≤ −150 dBc limit and the result is on the report. Units ordered without that grade are not sold as low-PIM parts.
