What a buyer can hold us to

Indoor DAS, repeater remotes, and band cleanup around LTE and 5 GHz Wi-Fi.

Numbers you can test

Insertion loss, ripple, VSWR and rejection on the datasheet are the limits from the factory specification for that model. Return loss is the calculated equivalent of VSWR ≤ 1.3.

A test report leaves with the unit

Tuning is done on a vector network analyzer. The report covers the electrical rows. Low PIM at ≤ −150 dBc @ 2×43 dBm is an order option, measured when it is ordered.

Drawings, not slogans

Outline drawings from the specification are printed on the datasheet, with mounting holes where the drawing states them.

Small-batch custom bands

Send the band plan, rejection, power, connector and envelope. The quotation states the lead time. We do not print a universal lead time here.
