About the factory

Shenzhen Kangxuntong, a cavity-filter factory.

The company

深圳市康讯通技术有限公司, in English Shenzhen Kangxuntong Technology Co., Ltd., builds cavity filters in Shenzhen. The work is bandpass filters, diplexers and multi-band combiners for LTE, 5G indoor radio and Wi-Fi cleanup.

The factory

Tuning, assembly and outgoing test are done in the Shenzhen workshop. Photographs on this site are of that workshop and of current hardware.
