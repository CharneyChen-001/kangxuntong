Privacy

How this site handles an enquiry.

When you press “Send enquiry”, the quote form sends what you typed to our sales mailbox, sales@cavityfilter.com, through the form relay service FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), which handles it under its own terms. If you choose WhatsApp or email instead, the text is sent by that service under its own terms.

We use your name, company, phone number and email only to answer the enquiry. We do not sell them or pass them to third parties. To have an enquiry deleted, write to sales@cavityfilter.com.
