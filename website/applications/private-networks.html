Applications / Private radio networks

Private Radio Networks: TETRA, P25 and DMR

Public safety, utilities, transport and industrial sites run their own two-way radio (LMR / PMR) networks. Cavity filters sit at the base station and repeater sites of these networks.

Where the systems operate

Frequencies are set by each country’s regulator and each licence. The usual ranges are:

SystemStandardTypical bands
TETRAETSI EN 300 392380–400 MHz (public safety in many countries), 410–430 MHz, 450–470 MHz; 800 MHz in some regions
P25 (APCO-25)TIA-102VHF 136–174 MHz, UHF 380–512 MHz, 700 MHz and 800 MHz public-safety bands
DMRETSI TS 102 361VHF 136–174 MHz, UHF 400–470 MHz

Where cavity filters go

  • Repeater duplexer. Transmit and receive share one antenna. The duplexer passes each half of the pair and isolates the receiver from the transmitter at the duplex spacing.
  • Receiver protection. A bandpass cavity in front of the receiver rejects nearby transmitters on shared towers.
  • Transmitter combining. Multi-channel and trunked sites combine several transmitters onto one antenna.
  • Coexistence with cellular. Band filters keep LTE and 5G energy out of the radio path, and the other way round.
