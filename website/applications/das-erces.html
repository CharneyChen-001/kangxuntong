Applications / In-building DAS and ERCES

In-building DAS and Emergency Responder Radio (ERCES)

A distributed antenna system (DAS) carries cellular and public-safety radio signals inside a building. Cavity filters decide which bands enter it and keep them apart.

Emergency responder radio coverage

In the United States many jurisdictions require in-building coverage for fire and police radio. The requirement comes from the International Fire Code (section 510) and NFPA 1225, as adopted locally. The authority having jurisdiction (AHJ) sets the frequencies, the coverage targets and the acceptance test. System listings and acceptance are handled by the system integrator with the AHJ.

Where cavity filters go

  • Signal booster / BDA band selection. Pass the licensed channels, reject adjacent services.
  • Point of interface (POI). Band combiners and duplexers join several operators’ signal sources onto the DAS.
  • Shared cabling. Combiners and diplexers separate public-safety and cellular systems on one riser.
  • Low PIM. Passive intermodulation from strong downlink carriers can land in uplink bands, so low-PIM parts matter at the POI. Low PIM is an order option on our datasheets.
